# YanXi for TuneLab

嫣汐（YanXi）as a singing voice inside TuneLab.

- **Ready to sing after install** — the YanXi voice bank ships inside this extension,
  so no separate MUTA installation is needed.
- Chinese and Japanese from the same voice: the language is a part setting.
- Pitch, vibrato, automation, pinned phonemes, per-note sample-range shift and manual
  breaths are all driven by the TuneLab editor.
- Built-in worldline R1.1 vocoder — no external neural vocoder to configure.
- Want another MUTA bank too? Add its folder under *Settings → Extensions → YanXi*
  and it shows up in the voice list next to 嫣汐.

Engine: a fork of [MUTA for TuneLab](https://github.com/KakaruHayate/MUTAForTuneLab) (MIT).
